## Interpretation

The result is consistent across every GPU measurement collected today:

1. `blockwise` is the only competitive decode family on the tested H100 shapes.
2. Current Mosaic families are not losing by a narrow tuning margin. They are losing by structure.
3. The eager KV-update matrix does not support promoting `compact_scatter` or `sorted_compact_scatter`.

Concrete structural reasons in the live code:

- `throughput` is still a split/broadcast/merge bridge on top of the batched Mosaic core, not a native long-context decode operator: [mosaic_gpu_attention.py](/home/saran-gangster/Desktop/Implementations%20&%20Projects/jax/nano-vllm/src/nanovllm_jax/layers/mosaic_gpu_attention.py)
- `latency` follows the same wrapper pattern with different partitioning, so it inherits the same bridge overhead: [mosaic_gpu_attention.py](/home/saran-gangster/Desktop/Implementations%20&%20Projects/jax/nano-vllm/src/nanovllm_jax/layers/mosaic_gpu_attention.py)
- the batched Mosaic core is metadata-heavy and synchronization-heavy compared with the streaming blockwise operator: [mosaic_gpu_attention.py](/home/saran-gangster/Desktop/Implementations%20&%20Projects/jax/nano-vllm/src/nanovllm_jax/layers/mosaic_gpu_attention.py)
- `blockwise` is structurally simpler: single streaming online-softmax, no split batch, no JAX-side partial reduction: [paged_attention.py](/home/saran-gangster/Desktop/Implementations%20&%20Projects/jax/nano-vllm/src/nanovllm_jax/layers/paged_attention.py)

Practical implication:

- do not promote current Mosaic `baseline`, `latency`, or `throughput`
- do not promote compact KV-update backends from today’s data
- the next meaningful GPU work should be structural (`throughput-v2`, kernel ownership, reduction boundary removal), not more narrow geometry tuning on the current bridge design
