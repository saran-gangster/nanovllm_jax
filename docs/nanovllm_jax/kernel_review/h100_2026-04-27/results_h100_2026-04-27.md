## Remaining Limits

This result does not justify:

- removing the env gate
- generalizing to other dtypes
- generalizing to other GQA ratios
- generalizing beyond the 10 validated rows
- claiming non-H100 portability without another run

The next GPU validation target is a second hardware pass on an H100 NVL-class pod or another production H100 to confirm portability across H100 variants.
